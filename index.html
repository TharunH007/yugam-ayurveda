Our services

Seven ways of working, one diagnosis

The doctor reads the case first — pulse, history, the way you stand and sleep — and only then chooses the therapy. Most patients receive a combination over a short course.

01

Ayurveda Panchakarma

The five classical purificatory procedures, given as a supervised course — the practice at the centre of everything we do.

02

Acupuncture

Fine sterile needling at diagnosed points — used most for migraine, sciatica, frozen joints and nerve pain.

03

Cupping therapy

Suction cups to draw stagnation out of tight muscle — shoulders, upper back and the lumbar region respond quickly.

04

Foot reflexology

Pressure work on the sole's reflex zones — for heel pain, poor sleep, and the sluggishness that follows illness.

05

Varma

The Tamil tradition of vital-point manipulation, given by hand — for old injuries, stiffness and pain that has settled deep.

06

Chiropractic

Careful adjustment of the spine and joints, combined with oil therapy so the correction holds.

07

Detoxification

A programme for body and mind together — diet, medicated oils, herbal preparations and rest, prescribed for your constitution.

The doctor

Dr. R. Yuvaraj

A graduate in Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery with a diploma in acupuncture, Dr. Yuvaraj has built a practice around one belief: chronic pain rarely has a single cause, and it rarely answers to a single treatment. Every patient is examined at length before anything is prescribed.

Patients travel to the clinic from across Thiruvannamalai district and from among the pilgrims who come to the hill — many after being told that surgery was the only remaining option.
